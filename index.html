ChipLog

Log your poker sessions. Know where you actually stand.

ChipLog is an iPhone app for cash-game and tournament players who want an honest record of their results — what you played, how long you sat, and what it did to your bankroll.

What it does

  • Log a session in seconds — cash or tournament, with stakes, buy-ins, top-ups, rebuys, add-ons and what you cashed out.
  • Run a live session — start the timer at the table, add top-ups as you go, count your stack to see where you stand, and end it when you rack up. Closing the app doesn't stop the clock.
  • See the trend — a cumulative profit chart plus profit, $/hr, hours, win rate and session count, for this week, month, year, all time or a range you pick.
  • In the units that actually compare — bb/hr for cash, so 8 bb/hr at $1/$2 counts as the same achievement as at $5/$10, plus ROI and ITM% for tournaments.
  • Find where the money comes from — the same numbers broken down by stakes, game, venue and format.
  • Track your bankroll — sessions plus your own deposits, withdrawals and adjustments, so the number you see is the real one.
  • Keep it tidy — your own list of game types, venues and tags, notes on any session, and search and filters over the whole history.
  • Take your data with you — export every session to CSV, or import one. ChipLog reads its own exports and PokerBankrollTracker's, and skips sessions you already have.
  • Your data stays yours — everything lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud account. There's no ChipLog account and no ChipLog server.
