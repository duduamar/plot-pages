ChipLog

Log your poker sessions. Know where you actually stand.

ChipLog is an iPhone app for cash-game and tournament players who want an honest record of their results — what you played, how long you sat, and what it did to your bankroll.

What it does

  • Log a session in seconds — cash or tournament, with stakes, buy-ins, rebuys and cash-out.
  • Run a live session — start a timer at the table, add rebuys as you go, and end it when you rack up. It survives closing the app.
  • See the trend — cumulative profit over time, hourly rate, bb/hr, tournament ROI and ITM%, broken down by stake, game, location and format.
  • Track your bankroll — sessions plus deposits and withdrawals, so the number you see is the real one.
  • Your data stays yours — everything lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud account. There's no ChipLog account and no ChipLog server.
