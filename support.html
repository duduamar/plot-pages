Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and — if you're signed into iCloud — in your own private iCloud storage, which is what syncs it between your devices. There's no ChipLog account and no ChipLog server. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I move my sessions across?

Sign into the same iCloud account and install ChipLog — your sessions sync down on their own. If you don't use iCloud, export your sessions to CSV first and import them on the new device.

Does a live session keep running if I close the app?

Yes. A live session records when it started, so its timer is correct when you come back — even if the app was closed or your phone restarted.

How do I get rid of the ads?

There's a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in Settings. It's tied to your Apple Account, so "Restore Purchases" brings it back on a new device.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. To remove the synced copy as well, delete ChipLog's data from your iCloud settings.
