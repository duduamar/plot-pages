Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and — if you're signed into iCloud — in your own private iCloud storage, which is what syncs it between your devices. There's no ChipLog account and no ChipLog server. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I move my sessions across?

Sign into the same iCloud account and install ChipLog — your sessions sync down on their own. If you don't use iCloud, export your sessions to CSV first (Settings › Data) and import that file on the new device.

Does a live session keep running if I close the app?

Yes. A live session records when it started, so its timer is correct when you come back — even if the app was closed or your phone restarted. While it's running you can add top-ups and update your current stack; the session finishes when you tap End Session and enter what you cashed out.

Can I import my history from another tracker?

Settings › Data › Import sessions (CSV) reads two kinds of file, and works out which one you picked on its own:

  • A CSV that ChipLog exported — from another device, or as a backup.
  • A PokerBankrollTracker export.

You get a preview of what will be added before anything is written, and a row the file can't make sense of is reported rather than silently dropped. Sessions you already have are skipped, so re-importing the same file is safe. Coming from a tracker that isn't on this list? Email me the export format — it's the kind of thing worth adding.

Where do my exported sessions go?

Settings › Data › Export sessions (CSV) hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet, so you choose — Files, Mail, another app. The file is a plain .csv that opens in Numbers or Excel.

How do I get rid of the ads?

There's a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in Settings. It's tied to your Apple Account, so "Restore Purchases" brings it back on a new device. If you decline the tracking prompt you'll still see ads — they're just generic ones rather than ones chosen for you.

Can I use more than one currency?

ChipLog uses a single currency at a time, set in Settings. Sessions already logged keep the currency they were played in, so switching doesn't rewrite your history.

Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Not today — ChipLog is an iPhone app, portrait only. It needs iOS 26 or later.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Data › Reset erases your sessions, and optionally your venues, game types and bankroll entries with them. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. To remove the synced copy as well, delete ChipLog's data from your iCloud settings.
